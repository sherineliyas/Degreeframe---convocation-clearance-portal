Passport size photo requirements for Degree Certificate
Important Note: Photographs that do not comply with these instructions will be rejected by the review committee.
  • The photograph should be in colour and of the size of (35 mm x 40 mm).
  • Image size should be less than 80 KB.
  • Clear Image must be in JPEG format.
  • Do not upload self-taken photographs or selfies.
  • Photographs should be formal and taken in proper studio.
  • It should have full face, front view, eyes open.
  • Photo should present full head from top of hair to bottom of chin.
  • Center head within frame.
  • The photo should have a plain light-coloured background.
  • There should not be any distracting shadows, flash reflections on the face or in the background.
  • Head coverings are not permitted except for religious reasons, but the facial features from bottom of chin to top of forehead and both edges of the face must be clearly shown.
  • The expression on the face should look natural (Neutral & mouth closed).
